# Garage inventory surface

## Scope and mode

Replace the visual identity of `server/src/main/resources/web/index.html`. Operate mode. Preserve the vehicle CRUD workflow, API fields, search, demo mode, and English interface copy.

## Audience and task

An enthusiast with a personal garage or a dealer with stock needs to scan vehicles, find a registration, add a record, and edit or remove one without losing context. The browser screen must show inventory state immediately and keep the primary action obvious.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A modern vehicle registry with the care of a coachbuilder's leather-bound archive; the interface remains an efficient working app, never a generic grid of white cards or a literal paper ledger.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep oxblood and graphite, warm ivory, restrained burnished copper; editorial serif for identity and vehicle names, precise sans for controls and readable data. Dense, aligned rows carry the collection. One original automotive photograph adds material and atmosphere.

**STORY:** See the size and health of the collection, scan or search the inventory, then open a clear record editor. Empty and failed states explain the next action.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact dark masthead with the name, collection count, connection state and Add vehicle action; an editorial image strip establishes the world without burying the task; below it, search and the inventory lead. On wide screens the selected record or editor sits beside the collection; on mobile it follows in a focused drawer. The signature move is a vehicle dossier: selecting a row promotes its make, model and registration into a legible detail header while leaving the list visible.

**FORM:** Coachbuilder archive, candidate three in the grounded automotive list. `seed: none (unavailable)` because the roll did not print a 16-digit seed reference. Operate mode limits the archive to type, palette, density and the dossier move; navigation and controls stay familiar web UI.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
